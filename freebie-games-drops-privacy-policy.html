Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Freebie Games & Drops collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

Last updated: April 14, 2026

1. Overview

Freebie Games & Drops is operated by Epic Freebie Games Loot Radar / MSS Dev. The app package name is com.lootradar.loot_radar. The app helps users discover gaming giveaways, free-to-play titles, subscription rewards, Twitch Drops, DLC, packs, trials, betas, playtests, and other limited-time rewards across different platforms and stores.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect limited information necessary to operate and improve the app, including:

  • Technical information such as device type, app version, operating system, and language settings.
  • Push notification tokens used to send alerts about new offers and drops.
  • Advertising-related data provided by Google AdMob to display rewarded ads.
  • Basic usage and diagnostic information needed to improve app stability, performance, and security.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment card data, private messages, or precise location.

3. How We Use Information

  • To load and display live offers, drops, and promotions.
  • To send push notifications about new gaming offers, drops, and rewards.

  • To maintain app performance, reliability, abuse prevention, and security.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services, including:

These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services you interact with.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share limited technical data when required to operate app features such as notifications, ads, analytics, diagnostics, or external store links.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information used by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Freebie Games & Drops is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review and remove it where required.
